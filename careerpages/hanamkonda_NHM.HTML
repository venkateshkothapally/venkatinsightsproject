Age Limits & Relaxations

Minimum age: 18 years. Maximum age: 44 years as on 01.07.2026, as per rules .

  • SC / ST / BC / EWS5 years relaxation
  • Ex-Servicemen3 years + length of service (Medical Officers)
  • NCC Instructors3 years + length of service in NCC
  • Persons with Disabilities10 years relaxation
3

Application Fee Details

Others / General: ₹500/-

SC, ST, PH: ₹250/-

Payment must be in the shape of a Demand Draft (DD) in favour of DM&HO, Hanumakonda .

SELECTION PROCESS

Method of Recruitment

01

Selection Criteria

  • Merit basis out of 100 marks .
  • 90 marks allotted for marks obtained in qualifying examination .
  • 10 marks for age weightage (0.5 mark per completed year above 18, max 10 marks, reckoned as on 01.07.2026) .
02

Reservation & Rules

  • Community, disability, and ex-service reservation strictly followed .
  • 33 1/3% women reservation applicable .
  • Local reservations as per Presidential Orders and G.O.Ms.No.124.
  • Scheduled Area vacancies under G.O.68 reserved for local Scheduled Tribes .
03

General Conditions

  • Appointment is on contract/outsourcing basis for 1 year or until actual need ceases .
  • Panel of candidates utilized for future vacancies up to one year .
  • Selected candidates must maintain headquarters and cannot transfer .
BEFORE YOU APPLY

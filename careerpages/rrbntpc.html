The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have invited online applications under CEN No. 04/2026 for Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS) and, following Corrigendum-1, the newly added Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) post. Total vacancies stand at 4029 (3993 in the original CEN + 36 added via corrigendum). Applications are open from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026 (23:59 hrs) at rrbapply.gov.in.

CEN No. 04/2026 for Junior Engineer and Depot Material Superintendent was published on official RRB websites on 13 August 2026. On 18 August 2026, RRBs issued Corrigendum-1, which added a new post - Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) - along with 35 additional CMA vacancies, one extra JE/Electrical/TRS vacancy at RRB Secunderabad, a new Medical Standard (B-2), and the CMA exam syllabus. The notification is now referred to as CEN No. 04/2026 (JE/DMS/CMA). Online applications are open from 14 August 2026 to 13 September 2026.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official RRB website or rrbapply.gov.in and Create an Account (mandatory personal mobile number and email ID; these cannot be changed later).
  2. Log in and select CEN No. 04/2026 (JE/DMS/CMA).
  3. Choose one RRB only and indicate zone/post preferences in order - this choice is final and cannot be changed.
  4. Fill in personal, educational and category details carefully.
  5. Complete live photo capture (webcam/front camera) as per the prescribed guidelines; upload signature (JPG/JPEG, 30–49 KB).
  6. Upload SC/ST certificate (PDF, under 400 KB) if opting for free train travel facility.
  7. Pay the applicable examination fee online (internet banking / debit-credit card / UPI).
  8. Submit the application; note down your Registration Number.
  9. Use the modification window (16.09.2026 to 25.09.2026) if corrections are needed, on payment of ₹250 per modification.

Documents Required

  • Educational qualification certificates (diploma/degree, as applicable to the post) with date of issue
  • Matriculation/10th certificate (for name, DOB, father's/mother's name)
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/EBC), in prescribed Annexure format, from a competent authority
  • Ex-Servicemen declaration/employment certificate, where applicable
  • PwBD disability certificate and UDID, where applicable
  • Valid original photo ID (Government-issued photo identification, Voter ID, PAN, passport, driving licence, etc.)
  • Recent colour passport-size photograph and running-handwriting signature
  • NOC from present employer (for those serving in Government/PSU), where applicable

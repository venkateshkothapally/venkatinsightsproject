SELECTION PROCESS

Method of Recruitment

01

MLHP Posts

  • Municipal HWCs: MBBS / BAMS doctors only, with MBBS priority.
  • Non-municipal HWCs: MBBS, then BAMS, then eligible B.Sc Nursing / GNM candidates with CPCH.
  • Merit based on combined percentage in qualifying examinations.
  • Induction / bridge training as specified in the guidelines.
02

Other Posts

  • Selection on merit out of 100 marks.
  • 90 marks based on qualifying examination marks.
  • 10 marks age weightage: 0.5 per completed year above 18, as on 01.07.2026.
  • Merit lists and objection process published on the official website.
03

Reservation Rules

  • Community, disability and ex-service reservation as per applicable rules.
  • 33⅓% reservation for women as cited in the notification.
  • Local reservation as per Presidential Order 2018 and relevant GOs.
  • Scheduled Area vacancies under G.O.68 reserved for local Scheduled Tribes.

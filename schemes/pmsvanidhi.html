Key Features

  • Loan Tranches: Get collateral-free loans in three progressive steps—up to ₹15,000, ₹25,000, and ₹50,000.
  • Interest Subsidy: Borrowers get a 7% interest subsidy per year for timely repayment.
  • Digital Incentives: Cashbacks of up to ₹1,200 to ₹1,600 per year for digital transactions.
  • Credit Cards: Eligible vendors can get a UPI-linked RuPay credit card with a limit of up to ₹30,000.

Objectives

  • Help street vendors restart their businesses post COVID-19.
  • Bring informal workers into the formal banking system.
  • Promote digital transactions and financial literacy.
  • Extend social security benefits to vendor families.

Who is Eligible?

Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) Requirements:

  • Individuals: Sole proprietors, self-employed workers, artisans, shopkeepers.
  • Groups: Self-help groups (SHGs), proprietary concerns, etc.
  • Age Limit: Must be between 18 and 65 years old.
  • Sector: Non-corporate, non-farm enterprise engaged in manufacturing, trading, or services (agriculture allied activities allowed).

How to Apply Online

  1. Verify mobile number: Ensure your phone number is linked to your Aadhaar. Request an OTP on the portal.
  2. Provide details: Choose vendor category and enter SRN or apply for an LoR.
  3. Submit documents: Upload KYC documents (Aadhaar card) and proof of vending.
  4. Get Help: You can apply via local Urban Local Body (ULB) office or a Common Service Centre (CSC).
